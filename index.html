100% free data, no account or key needed. Gold: spot XAU/USD from Coinbase's public exchange rates when available, otherwise PAXG (a token backed 1:1 by a London Good Delivery gold ounce that tracks spot closely), with Binance as a last backup. Dollar: euro rate from the same feed. Trades and order book: Coinbase PAXG market. News: GDELT, scored by keywords. Prices refresh every minute; news every 10 minutes.
Forecasts are statistical estimates from momentum, dollar moves, order flow and news tone, with ranges sized from recent volatility. Short-term gold moves are mostly noise; this is a decision aid, not financial advice.
